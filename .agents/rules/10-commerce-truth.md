# Commerce Truth Rule

All public commerce claims must come from authoritative data/configuration.

Never fabricate ratings, reviews, popularity, sales, scarcity, stock quantity, delivery dates, shipping thresholds, warranty/return periods, discounts, certifications or customer counts.

A section title manually chosen by the merchant may contain marketing language, but the system must not automatically assert unsupported claims such as Best Seller or Trending.

For Pakistan/COD operations, treat delivered orders and delivered revenue as the strongest commerce-success truth when relevant. Preserve existing status definitions and authoritative order calculations.

If a value is absent, hide it or render a neutral state rather than inventing it.
