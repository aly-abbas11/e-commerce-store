# Storefront Retail

Optimize for discovery, decision confidence, conversion and trust — not visual novelty.

## Homepage hierarchy

Prefer clear category discovery near the top, then truthful value/trust communication and product merchandising. Every section needs a customer or commercial purpose.

## Product merchandising

Image first, title readable, price obvious, real reviews only, truthful sale state, clear Add to Cart/Select Options behavior. Align card anatomy across grids. Avoid heavy card chrome.

## Electronics credibility

Real model/specification data, compatibility, connectivity/capacity/power facts when structured, useful policy/support paths and genuine product media build trust better than badges.

## Anti-AI-slop review

Remove gratuitous glow, glassmorphism, gradients, blobs, generic copy, fake social proof and repetitive rounded containers.

## QA

Inspect real rendered pages at ~1440px and ~390px. Check first viewport, category discovery, card density, image crop, CTA alignment, overflow, keyboard/touch behavior and console/runtime errors.
