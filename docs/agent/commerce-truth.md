# VoltGear Commerce Truth

## Market

Primary market context is Pakistan. Currency is PKR. COD is a central operating reality when enabled by current configuration.

## Order truth

Do not equate placed orders with realized commerce. Where analytics/operations require a sales truth, distinguish the existing order lifecycle and especially delivered outcomes. Never silently redefine statuses.

## Product truth

Prices, compare prices, variants, availability and stock must come from authoritative product/inventory contracts. Never infer a discount merely from marketing text.

## Trust truth

Shipping, return, warranty, support, COD and payment claims must come from store settings/policies/current functionality. If missing, hide rather than invent.

## Review truth

Only genuine non-demo/public review data may drive ratings, counts or structured-data claims.

## Merchandising truth

Admin may curate section names and products. Automated labels like Best Seller, Trending or Popular require actual supporting data and defined logic.
